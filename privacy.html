LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy policy.

AK STAR GLOBAL respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

01 / Information We Collect

When you submit a project enquiry through our website, we collect your name, email address, selected service category and project message. Please avoid sharing passwords, financial credentials or sensitive personal data in enquiry forms.

02 / How We Use Your Information

We use your submitted details strictly to review your project requirements, respond to your enquiry, prepare service quotes and deliver requested creative or technical work. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

03 / Data Storage & Security

Enquiry records are stored using secure cloud infrastructure with restricted administrative access. While we follow practical security safeguards, no internet transmission is completely immune to risk.

04 / Your Rights & Contact

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your submitted enquiry records at any time by reaching out through our Contact page.

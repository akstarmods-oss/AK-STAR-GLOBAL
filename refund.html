BILLING & CANCELLATIONS

Refund policy.

Transparent cancellation and refund terms for custom creative, development and digital product orders at AK STAR GLOBAL.

01 / Custom Creative & Development Services

Because design, video, audio, app and web projects involve dedicated production hours, advance deposits are non-refundable once production work has commenced. If a project is cancelled before work starts, a full refund of the advance minus payment gateway fees is issued.

02 / Milestone-Based Projects

For multi-stage software, web or app projects, payments are tied to completed milestones. If either party terminates a project mid-way, completed and delivered milestones remain payable while unstarted future milestones are not billed.

03 / Digital Products & Downloads

Downloadable templates, presets and digital assets are non-refundable once accessed or downloaded, except in the case of a verified corrupted file that cannot be replaced.

04 / Support & Resolution

If you experience any issue with a delivery, contact us via our Contact page within 7 days of handover so our team can resolve or revise it promptly.
